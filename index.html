Solar System Explorer - Free Interactive 3D Solar System Simulator Online

Explore the Planets, Moons, and Spacecraft in Stunning 3D

Welcome to the Solar System Explorer, the most advanced free online 3D solar system simulator. Using WebGL and Three.js technology, we bring our cosmic neighborhood to life right in your browser.

What You Can Explore

  • The Sun - Our central star, a thermonuclear furnace
  • Mercury - The scorched innermost planet
  • Venus - Earth's twin gone wrong
  • Earth - Our home, the only known planet with life
  • Mars - The red planet, future of human exploration
  • Jupiter - The king of planets, a massive gas giant
  • Saturn - The ringed beauty of our solar system
  • Uranus - The sideways planet
  • Neptune - The windiest world
  • Pluto - The beloved dwarf planet
  • Voyager 1 & 2 - Humanity's most distant ambassadors

Perfect For

Students learning astronomy, teachers creating engaging lessons, space enthusiasts exploring the cosmos, parents educating children, and anyone curious about our universe.

Key Features

Interactive 3D visualization, real-time orbital mechanics, click planets to learn facts, guided 11-step tour, share views to social media, dark/light themes, mobile responsive design, high-quality graphics, completely free, no installation required.

Educational Value

Learn about planet masses, diameters, orbital periods, distances from the Sun, and fascinating facts about each celestial body. Perfect for school projects and astronomy education.
